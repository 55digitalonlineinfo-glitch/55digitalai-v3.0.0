How the new titles and descriptions were built

Three data sources, and every change is backed by numbers or by the product page.

1

Keyword Planner

We checked how often customers search for phrases describing the products. This shows what customers call the product.

2

Google Ads search terms report

48 210 real queries from your campaigns with cost, conversions and conversion value. This shows which words sell, not just which are popular.

3

Checked against the product page

Every sentence of the new description was checked against the product page. Descriptions contain only features confirmed on the page.

New title structure
Best-selling phrase+Brand and model+Key feature

Bean to cup coffee machine with grinder Demo Brand A300 Milk | with milk frother, 15 bar

  • The most important phrases go in the first ~50 characters, because that is usually all Google shows in a Shopping ad.
  • The brand stays in the visible part of the title, but after the phrase that sells.
  • Every title stays within the 150-character limit.
  • Descriptions: main phrase first, supporting phrases once each, no promotional claims.
